# Future-state requirements and objectives

These ideas are intentionally preserved outside the alpha so they remain visible without expanding the current delivery risk.

## Product expansion

- Versioned catalog adapters for Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude, followed by live normalized pricing feeds across multiple providers.
- A pinned-model watchlist with price, capability, latency, and deprecation alerts.
- Recommendations that use measured evaluation scores rather than broad quality tiers.
- Workload import from IDEs, agent traces, invoices, and observability platforms.
- Scenario portfolios for teams, environments, and growth forecasts.
- Cost coverage for storage, retrieval, tools, web search, fine-tuning, infrastructure, and human review.
- Historical pricing and capability charts similar to a stock tracker.
- Personalized defaults synchronized across IDE and chat/LLM workspaces.

## Network and value model

- Opt-in, privacy-preserving benchmarks contributed by builders.
- Small transaction or savings-based fees—potentially a tiny fraction of optimized spend—rather than a large seat license.
- A “network of networks” where better workload and evaluation signals improve recommendations for participants.
- Value storage or rewards for contributors, subject to legal, accounting, privacy, and incentive-design review.

## Governance

- Provider-neutral ranking disclosures and auditable recommendation logic.
- Medical, financial, legal, and other high-stakes policy profiles.
- Organization controls for approved models, data residency, retention, and budget thresholds.
- Versioned pricing snapshots so every historical estimate remains reproducible.
- A reviewed-agent import path: license and provenance check, pinned commit, permission inspection, sandbox, adversarial eval, governance approval, and rollback.

Detailed priority, acceptance criteria, and provider expansion are maintained in [the backlog](artifacts/BACKLOG.md).
